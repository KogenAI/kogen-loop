The composition calls the real syscall.Kill, not an injected or mutable signal hook. Reject pgid <= 1 before calling kill. kill(-pgid, 0) returning nil or EPERM proves presence and returns alive without requiring a readable proc view. ESRCH alone never proves absence: enumerate the supplied FS. Any other kill error is an observation error. Enumeration failure is an observation error. Ignore nonnumeric entries. Skip numeric stat open/read failures matching ENOENT or ESRCH, including wrapped errors. Read pgrp after the final ')' in stat (comm can contain spaces and parentheses); all states including Z count. Remember other read errors (including EPERM/EACCES) and malformed stat rather than returning before a later readable membership witness. Membership wins over remembered errors. With no membership witness, any remembered error is an observation failure; only kill ESRCH plus a complete error-free no-member scan returns absent. Preserve errno wrapping for errors.Is checks.
